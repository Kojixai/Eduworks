the printed book) the website offers 10 NEW practice questions on exactly the same skill and curriculum point, auto-marked
where possible, each with a worked explanation. Questions must not duplicate the printed book's questions (same skill,
different numbers/contexts/wording). Same quality rules as the books: /home/claude/books/_shared/QUALITY_RULES.txt.
